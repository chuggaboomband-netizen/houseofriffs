CHUGGABOOM

ChuggaBoom is the Deadpool of metalcore

ChuggaBoom is a genre-bending metalcore band that bring a unique blend of humour, heavy music, and infectious energy to the stage. Hailing from Birmingham, UK, this group of talented musicians combine their love for metal and pop culture to create an unforgettable experience for fans.

Combining crushing breakdowns, infectious melodies, and their signature witty, tongue-in-cheek lyrics, ChuggaBoom deliver a live experience that leaves audiences headbanging, grinning, and fully locked into The Chugg Life.

In 2026, ChuggaBoom are carving out a bold new position in the scene with a new look, new music, and a renewed determination to spread The Chugg Life as far as it can go. It is the next chapter of the band, but with the same chaos, charisma, and unmistakable energy that made them stand out in the first place.
