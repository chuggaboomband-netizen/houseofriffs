About House of Riffs

House of Riffs is a booking agency built from real experience inside the live music scene.

With over 35 years of collective experience across bands, touring, promoting shows, artist development and live events, we understand what it takes to build the right opportunities for artists and deliver reliable, well-matched shows for promoters and venues.

We work with rock, metal and alternative artists who are serious about growing their audience, developing their live presence and making every show count.

Our role is to connect the dots - the right artists, the right rooms, the right promoters and the right strategy.

Good booking is about more than filling dates. It is about understanding the artist, the audience, the venue and the bigger picture. House of Riffs brings practical experience, industry knowledge and a genuine connection to the scene, offering artists and promoters a professional booking partner that understands live music from every angle.
